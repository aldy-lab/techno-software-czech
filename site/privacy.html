Privacy notice

This notice explains how Techno Software Czech s.r.o. handles personal data collected through this website, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Czech Act No. 110/2019 Coll.

Controller

Techno Software Czech s.r.o., Školská 1736/12, 110 00 Praha 1 – Nové Město, Czech Republic. IČO 29521513, registered with the Municipal Court in Prague, file C 447880.

What we collect

  • Contact requests. When you write to us, we receive your name, company, email address and the content of your message.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records technical data such as IP address and browser type for security and operation, kept for a short period.

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party trackers, fonts or embeds.

Why, and on what basis

We use contact data to answer your enquiry and prepare an offer (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, steps prior to a contract) and server logs to keep the site secure (Art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interest).

How long

Enquiries that do not lead to cooperation are deleted within 12 months. Data relating to a contract is kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Who processes it

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. Data shared with our partner Damas Engineering is limited to what is needed to staff your request, and only with your agreement.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and object to processing. You may also complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).
