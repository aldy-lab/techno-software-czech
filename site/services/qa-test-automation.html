What we staff

  • Test automation engineers — Playwright, Selenium, Cypress, and API test suites in the language your developers use.
  • Performance engineers — load and stress testing, and the profiling that follows a bad result.
  • QA analysts — test strategy, exploratory and acceptance testing, and test design for regulated releases.

Measured on every merge

Automated tests are only useful if they run on every change and people trust the result. Our engineers wire suites into your CI, keep them fast and stable, and treat a flaky test as a defect — so a red build means something.

Regulated releases

In banking, healthcare and automotive the evidence matters as much as the test. We staff people used to traceable test plans and reports that an auditor or a validation team can follow.
