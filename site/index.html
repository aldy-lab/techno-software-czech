Whowe are

Techno Software Czech is a Czech engineering company, born as a consulting-services spin-off of the well-established firm Damas Engineering Consulting s.r.o., that places senior software engineers, testers and DevOps specialists directly inside client teams.

You keep the roadmap, the rituals and the code ownership. We find the people, vet them technically, handle contracts and payroll, and stay accountable for how they perform. When the project grows, the team grows with it — when it settles, it scales back without layoffs on your side.

As part of the Damas Engineering group we reach a network of 500 specialists in five countries, so rare profiles — AUTOSAR, core-banking, safety-critical QA — are a search, not a gamble.

How we vet engineers
  1. 01

    Brief

    A call with your engineering lead about the stack, the team, the seniority and the way you work. We write it back as a role profile you approve.

    Day 1
  2. 02

    Shortlist

    We search our bench and the Damas Engineering network, run technical screening, and send you a short list of people we would hire ourselves.

    Within a week
  3. 03

    Interview & onboard

    You interview, you decide. We handle the contract, equipment and access, and the engineer joins your next sprint planning.

    2 – 4 weeks
  4. 04

    Scale & support

    Regular check-ins with you and the engineer, performance feedback, and a replacement if the fit is not right. Add or release people month by month.

    Ongoing
